# Kurkov's binary Stirling row edge and extensions of Ehrenborg–Steingrímsson and Erlbacher

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

We prove Kurkov's row-edge conjecture in OEIS A358612, revision 52, FORMULA, first conjectured formula, for every nonnegative row index, and give a polynomial refinement of Ehrenborg and Steingrímsson's excedance formulas (2000, Lemma 6.2, Theorem 6.3 and Proposition 6.5) extending the binary-product consequences in Erlbacher's note (July 2026, Section 2 and Theorem 3.1). The last entry is A284005(n)/(1+wt(n))!, where wt is binary weight. A dyadic block formula gives integral positive support and identifies the complete row with a rising-factorial expansion of the polynomials in A373183. This proves the signed factorial companion in A358612, two Stirling-basis identities in A373183, and the conjectured identity in MathOverflow question 483753. We also prove the lower-bit and binomial identities in A284005 and A341392, rederive the known Boolean transform of excedance counts, and characterize equality in a sharp lower bound. The printed unrestricted suffix identities fail; corrected statements hold for 0<=q<2^m, with a nonzero prefix in the generalized initial-factorial version.
